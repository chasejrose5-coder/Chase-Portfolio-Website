Intro

I’m Chase Rose, a senior at Bentley Upper School in Lafayette, California, where I have served as a three-year captain of both the track & field and soccer teams. I have set school records in both the 400m and 800m, earned multiple All-League honors, and was named Team MVP in both sports. In the 2026 soccer season I was named League Offensive Player of the Year and BCL Championship MVP. Aside from sports, I participate in student government, paper research, and building programs to fix issues that help athletes.

Outside of sports I founded a local mobile detailing business, wrote a research thesis on West African soccer academies (JRP Honorable Mention), and am currently building an 800m training app. I care about leadership that actually shows up, whether that’s being part of a relay, managing a student store and its inventory, interviewing nationally recognized figures on stage, or organizing community service. I’m looking to carry that same standard into college and beyond.
